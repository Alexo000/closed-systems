Urna: terms of sale

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Who runs it. Urna is an artwork by Alejandro Ramos Saavedra, 89A King Street, London W6 9HW. Write to alejandro@ramos-saavedra.com.

What you pay for. An ampoule: one place in a simulated box, filled with the choice you make. It is an online artwork. Nothing is posted to you, and a payment buys no share in the box or its money.

Price. Shown on the page in pounds. It is set each morning and is the same for everyone that day. Stripe takes the payment.

What happens next. Your ampoule is filled at the next daily step and breaks on a secret day within 45 days. The next morning your alias and the price go in the public log, where they stay, so choose an alias you are happy to see in public. Your choice stays sealed until the first opening after the ampoule breaks.

Cancelling. You ask for your ampoule to start straight away. Once it is filled, you lose your right to cancel and the payment cannot be refunded.

If the box is already closed. A payment that arrives while the box is closed breaks no ampoule and is refunded.

If the box closes first. An ampoule that has not broken when the box closes never breaks, and its payment is not refunded.

Old links. If you pay less than the day's price through an old link, it counts as a donation and breaks no ampoule.

Gifts. A gift for the next box breaks no ampoule. If gifts reach the target, the next box starts. If they never do, the money goes to building the real box.

No promises about the box. The box is a simulation that runs on chance. I make no promise about what any ampoule will do to it.

Your details. See the privacy page.

Your rights. Nothing here takes away your legal rights. English law applies.
