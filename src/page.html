The rules
  • Price. Starts at £1. It doubles with every ampoule broken and drops about 9% each week with none.
  • Secret. Your alias and the price go in the log. Your choice, and the day it breaks, are sealed with a code published the same day. The break day is random, between 1 and 45 days later, so nobody can tell whose ampoule just broke. The salt in the water only shows in bands.
  • Openings. Every 261 days the urn opens. Every choice is shown, and anyone can check it against its seal.
  • Rent. The money pays the urn's own costs. What is left builds the real box. If a month can't be paid, the urn closes for good.
  • Time. One day here is one week in the box. The lamp in the box is on from 07:00 to 21:00, London time.
